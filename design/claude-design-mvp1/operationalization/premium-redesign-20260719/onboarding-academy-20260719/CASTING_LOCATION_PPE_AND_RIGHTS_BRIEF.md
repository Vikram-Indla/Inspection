**Document ID:** OBM-RIGHTS-20260719-001
**Status:** CONTROLLED PROPOSAL — procurement/production brief. Nothing here is contracted; all items `MEDIA_DEP`; budget, vendor and brand approvals are `SPONSOR_DECISION`.

## 1. Casting

- **Roles to cast (on-camera):** Inspector (male and female variants), Reviewer, Planner, Operations officer, Admin/steward, Auditor, Factory Representative, Leadership figure, and background factory staff. Committee figures only after role reconciliation (`SPONSOR_DECISION`).
- **Representation:** credible Saudi professionals; both men and women in professional roles, consistent with the source storyboard reference (male professionals in thobe/ghutra; female professionals in abaya/hijab; field staff in workwear). Dress choices for women on the factory floor must combine cultural correctness with PPE compliance (hijab-compatible hard hats/hearing protection). No caricature, no tokenism; final mix is a casting decision reviewed by the sponsor.
- **Language ability:** Arabic-first delivery; EN capability preferred for dual-track shoots, otherwise separate EN VO.
- **No real employees' identities** unless they sign the same releases as talent; no real names/badges readable on camera.

## 2. Locations

- Factory floor + industrial-city exterior (e.g., MODON-style estate), government office, operations room, committee room. Actual site selection requires location agreements and, for government interiors, sponsor facilitation (`SPONSOR_DECISION`).
- Every location: written location release, safety induction for crew, restricted-area rules, and a no-sensitive-signage sweep (no real license plates, CR certificates, or third-party brands in frame without clearance).

## 3. PPE compliance (P0 — an incorrect-PPE frame is a reshoot)

| Zone depicted | Required minimum on camera |
|---|---|
| Factory production floor | Hard hat, hi-vis vest/jacket, safety footwear; eye/hearing protection where the depicted operation implies it (grinding, pressing, high-noise) |
| Electrical rooms (per storyboard p.18 style scenes) | Above + arc-appropriate distance; no open-panel touching on camera |
| Warehouse/yard | Hard hat, hi-vis, safety footwear |
| Office/committee/ops room | Business attire; no PPE props |
A qualified HSE reviewer signs each shot list and each final cut. Talent receives site safety induction; insurance covers crew and talent on industrial sites.

## 4. Rights & consent

- **Talent:** written appearance releases covering both AR and EN edits, all Academy distribution surfaces (in-product, internal training, sponsor demos), stills/posters derived from footage, and a defined term. Rights expiry dates are recorded in the content register; expiry forces unpublish (Bible §9).
- **Location:** signed releases per site; government-branded interiors additionally need sponsor/brand authority.
- **Brand marks:** ministry emblem and Vision 2030 marks appear in the source storyboards but require explicit written authorization before use in produced media (`SPONSOR_DECISION`).
- **Music/fonts/stock:** licensed only; license records stored with the content register.
- **Privacy:** PDPL-conscious handling of talent personal data; no real operational data, real factory identities, or real coordinates in any frame (fixture data only).

## 5. On-screen product truth during shoots

Screens filmed over-shoulder show the real product at a named commit on fixture data. Provider-pending surfaces are filmed as such (virtual room provider-pending; `AI briefing not enabled`; projected-movement label visible when operations map is in frame). The shoot supervisor carries the truth-label crib sheet from `FOUR_HOUR_PLATFORM_STORYLINE.md`.

## 6. Deliverables per shoot day

Raw footage + audio, bilingual caption/transcript source files, AD script, poster stills (per chapter), signed releases (talent/location), HSE sign-off sheet, and a shot-to-item mapping (shot → content-matrix item ID) so every frame is traceable.
