Use the master constitution. Cover SCR-WEB-100/110/120/130 and SB04. Inspect `apps/web/src/app/planning`, CriteriaBuilder, Wizard, ImmediateForm, factory data, risk presentation, and map usage.

Design three clearly distinct but consistent entry paths:

- Bulk: AND/OR criteria, progressive query building, eligible result count, table/map selection, invalid conditions, no matches, partial source failure, and saved draft.
- Single: factory search, identity confidence, Factory 360 preview, location/map verification, duplicate-visit warning, and continue.
- Immediate: urgency, registered/unregistered branch, minimum identity/location controls, restricted authorization, and dispatch readiness.

Risk is advisory and must show source/version rather than imply AI authority. Do not add Phase-2 AI features. Make method choice reversible until creation, preserve drafts, and show exact downstream consequences. Return desktop populated/empty/error/unauthorized designs, responsive behavior, Arabic/RTL, and the handoff contract into P02.
