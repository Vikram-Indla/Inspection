# P03 — Publish and Visit Management

Use the master constitution. Cover SCR-WEB-150/200/210, SB04/SB05, `planning/plans`, `visits`, calendar, workload, notes, attachments, and lifecycle actions.

Design two connected experiences:

1. Plan review and atomic publication: targets, child visits, assignments, package versions, validation results, notifications, duplicate/stale warnings, and per-plan audit. Partial publish is prohibited.
2. Operational management: dense searchable table, saved filters, KPI context, map/calendar/workload modes, bulk actions, detail dossier, timeline, notes, attachments, reschedule, reassign, return/republish, cancellation, and rule-driven expiry.

Required states include draft, validation blocked, publishing, published, returned, cancelled, expired, partially failed bulk action, stale row, unauthorized action, and degraded widget. Make business workflow status separate from journey operational state. Return desktop and constrained-width designs, Arabic/RTL behavior, keyboard table interaction, and the handoff into P04.
