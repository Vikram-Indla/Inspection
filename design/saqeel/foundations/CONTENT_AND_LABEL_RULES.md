# CONTENT_AND_LABEL_RULES
- Sentence case everywhere incl. buttons and column headers. No exclamation marks, no emoji.
- Actions are verb-led: "Assign inspector", "Submit for review", "Verify & close".
- Identifiers: mono, stable formats — INS-2026-004821, PRM-88213, VIO-/CAP-/FND- prefixes, coordinates 24.7136° N, 46.6753° E. Always LTR, even in Arabic copy.
- Dates: 20 Jul 2026 (EN) / 20 يوليو 2026 (AR, Latin digits preferred for cross-referencing).
- Empty states: state what's absent + the one next action. Errors: what happened + how to proceed; rejection always requires a reason.
- Arabic copy is written, not machine-mirrored: own leading, no letter-spacing, no uppercase transforms.
