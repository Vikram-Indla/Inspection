- Button: md=44px, lg=52px under field density; primary action duplicated in field taskbar.
- Input/Select/TextArea: 44px+ heights, 15px text; date/time use native pickers (touch-optimal).
- ChecklistQuestion: 44px seg options, evidence button adjacent.
- InspectionCard (queue variant): full-width touch cards replace register tables.
- FileUpload: camera-first capture affordance; EvidenceStack compact grid for review.
- SignaturePad (product component): re-chromed as FileUpload plate + signature kind.
- SyncIndicator + offline Alert: always visible in field chrome.
- Modal → full-width sheet ≤768px; Drawer covers 92vw.
