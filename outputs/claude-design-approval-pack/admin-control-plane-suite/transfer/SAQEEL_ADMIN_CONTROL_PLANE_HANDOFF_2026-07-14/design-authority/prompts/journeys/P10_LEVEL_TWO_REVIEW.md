# P10 — Level 2 Review and Decision

Use the master constitution plus the review/version prompt. Cover SCR-WEB-300/310 and SB10. Inspect reviews pages, DecisionPanel, report rendering, evidence access, immutable comments, risk/SLA data, assignment, and audit.

Design a reviewer queue optimized for prioritization by SLA, risk, age, assignment, version, and completeness. Design the review workspace as an evidence-led, read-only examination with persistent factory/submission/version context, section navigation, report/checklist, findings, violations, action forms, evidence gallery, timeline, and decision panel.

States: unclaimed, claimed, stale assignment, missing/degraded evidence, unauthorized, submitted version loading, approve, return exact sections with mandatory reason, reject with mandatory explanation, decision in progress, decided/locked, and failure without partial transition. Reviewer comments are immutable after decision.

Return desktop designs, Arabic/RTL, keyboard review navigation, partial-service isolation, decision safeguards, and P11 handoff.
