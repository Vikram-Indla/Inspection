# Saqeel Design Quality Ratchet V4

## Purpose

This ratchet prevents later Claude Design screens from becoming safer but more generic than earlier work. Coverage is necessary; visible decision superiority is also mandatory. Every new design must preserve the strongest accepted family grammar, contribute one justified page-specific interaction, and remain truthful to the implemented runtime.

## Non-negotiable gates

A design is returned before sponsor review if any gate fails:

1. **Runtime truth:** the route, object lifecycle, writable state, data source, provider and side effect are verified in code. A visual control cannot conceal a missing backend leg.
2. **Decision superiority:** the final composition demonstrably reduces decision time, hidden information, re-entry or irreversible-error risk compared with the current screen.
3. **Family continuity:** the accepted shell, tokens, typography, density, focus grammar, status language and existing family components are inherited. Each screen may introduce at most one new signature pattern.
4. **Evidence-based differentiation:** three hypotheses must differ at the primary decision zone, not only in card order or styling. The selected hypothesis must win against named criteria.
5. **Hard-case integrity:** conflicts, stale/concurrent state, unavailable dependencies, partial failure, unauthorized/read-only state and recovery are first-class frames, not footnotes.
6. **Arabic-first composition:** Arabic uses realistic long content, full-document RTL, mixed-direction identifiers and dates, correct physical order, and a narrow-layout proof.
7. **Accessible operation:** the primary decision works without a map, hover, drag-and-drop or color. Keyboard model, focus transfer, status/alert announcements and reduced motion are specified.
8. **Handoff truth:** every action maps through UI, guard, canonical transition, data/RLS, audit, side effect and test. Missing legs are `HANDOFF_BLOCKED`.

## Required design proof

Every CD submission must include:

- A current-screen critique naming the three highest-cost decision failures.
- A route-and-ownership memo before visual composition.
- A primary-source research ledger: observed principle, adopted treatment, rejected treatment and Saqeel-specific reason.
- Three equal-fidelity decision-zone hypotheses and a comparison against decision time, error prevention, evidence visibility, Arabic/RTL, keyboard burden and implementation truth.
- One selected full-page frame plus a close-up of the primary decision zone.
- One counterfactual frame with the signature interaction removed, explaining the measurable loss.
- Populated, loading, empty, invalid, unauthorized/read-only, stale/concurrent, degraded, submit-in-progress, failure-with-input-preserved and success states where applicable.
- Dark/light, Arabic RTL and narrow/mobile evidence using the same semantic state.
- An inheritance ledger: accepted components reused, page-specific components created, and regressions explicitly checked.
- A wiring map and exact file-level handoff. Design evidence must never claim runtime completion.

## Rejection triggers

- Generic card/dashboard assembly that could belong to another enterprise product after removing Saqeel labels.
- A score, recommendation, eligibility reason, freshness label, map distance or provider result without a verified source.
- Cosmetic variants presented as three hypotheses.
- A signature interaction that adds steps, animation or spectacle without reducing cognitive work or preventing error.
- Editing controls placed on a read-only or post-publish route.
- Self-awarded rubric scores or `5/5` claims.
- Lorem ipsum, generic names, impossible timestamps or clean-only demo data.
- Desktop-only density, mirrored-late Arabic, color-only conflicts, hover-only evidence or a map/timeline without an equivalent list/table path.
- Silent partial success, optimistic side effects, invented override policy or invented support/process information.

## Review cadence

- Run a drift audit after every three CDs and before each new family.
- Compare the new screen with the strongest accepted screen in its family, not merely with the current implementation.
- P2 observations are logged for the final Big Bang critique; P0/P1 findings block approval.
- Sponsor design approval is followed by an independent wiring audit. Implementation requires separate explicit authorization and runtime certification.
