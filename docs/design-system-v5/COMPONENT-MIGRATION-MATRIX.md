## Why Tabs and SignaturePad's modal weren't adopted
Modal is now adopted at 3 of 4 known raw-modal call sites (see above). Tabs remains unadopted because the ~6 `role="tab"` usages found were individually audited and are already correct as `<a>`/`<button>` in-page toggles, not a `Tabs.tsx` gap. `SignaturePad.tsx` was deliberately left on its own raw modal markup — it's the one remaining call site that combines a canvas ref, autosave-on-close timing, and legal-acknowledgement semantics in ways that deserve dedicated test coverage before changing its focus-trap/keyboard behavior, per CLAUDE.md's "never mark a page or component complete without runtime behavior, negative paths, tests, and evidence." This is recorded as an intentional boundary, not a silent gap.
