## Goal

## Decisions

**This wave is English-only, and that is the finding.** `scope` renders in
Arabic as **نطاق** — ordinary everyday Arabic for "range" or "area".
`نطاق التاريخ` is simply "date range"; `ضمن نطاقك` is "within your area". There
is no jargon on the Arabic side to remove. **The English was harder than its own
translation.**

That is worth stating plainly: for five waves the assumption has been that
Arabic inherits English's problems. Here it did not, because the translator
reached for a common word where the English author reached for a system word.
Changing the Arabic would have made it worse. Checked five samples before
deciding, and left them alone.

**54 of 84 resolved by rule, 30 by hand.** The repetitive ones —
`in your scope` · `your access scope` · `your authorized scope` ·
`your organizational scope` — all became **"your area"**. The 30 exceptions
carried real meaning and were written individually:

```
"Exact return scope"                 → "Exactly what to fix"
"Scope classification"               → "Change type"
"Expected (in returned scope)"       → "Expected (was asked for)"
"Date scope"                         → "Date range"
"A scope is required."               → "You must choose an area."
```

**The gate caught one more of mine** — a 16-word sentence in
`reviews.unauthorized.body`. Both languages fixed together. Seventh wave,
seventh catch on the author.
