A quiet, dense, bilingual system for national inspection work

SAQEEL serves field inspectors, supervisors and government stakeholders from one contract. Authority comes from hierarchy and restraint, not decoration. Colour is semantic, borders do the structural work, and Arabic is composed intentionally rather than mirrored mechanically. Lineage & influences

SAQEEL draws on two systems as references, not identities. Industry contributed structural discipline; Nocturne informed the dark theme. Neither system's visual identity — Industry's steel-blue, Barlow and blueprint registration marks; Nocturne's blurple accent and lighten-blend glow — survives here. What was borrowed is structure and behaviour, re-expressed in SAQEEL's own emerald-on-warm-neutral voice.

Brand & logo

The logo is symbol-first: a hexagonal inspection seal carrying a verification check — the language of certification and authority, in SAQEEL emerald. The wordmark is set in the system's own IBM Plex Sans at 600 with generous tracking; the name صقيل (ṣaqīl, “polished / burnished”) speaks to precision. The symbol always leads and can stand alone; the wordmark never appears as decoration.

Map workspace
