Fixture disclosure

Every numeric value shown in a "Live/Partial/Stale" KPI card below (compliance %, approval %, visit counts, etc.) is an illustrative design fixture for layout/density review — none is sourced from the repository or runtime data. No target, threshold, inspection-cycle rule, capacity threshold, workflow state, backend field or AI recommendation has been invented; where a policy is not published, the card renders Not configured or Decision required instead of a number, per the sponsor-approved fail-closed default.

Open governance decisions (blocking a Live state)

Per the Rectification Blueprint: (1) Health Score vs Risk Score are not synonyms — Risk Distribution needs a sponsor decision on which band this KPI reads. (2) Violation Trend needs a stored issuance timestamp (currently only submission time exists). (3) Coverage vs Annual Target needs a published inspection-cycle + target policy. (4) Repeat Violation Rate needs stable item lineage. (5) Risk-to-Attention needs a risk-snapshot rule (current band vs band-at-visit-time). (6) Checklist Items per Authority needs a reuse-counting rule. None of these are invented here.
