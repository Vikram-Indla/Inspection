# Baseline B1 — 5 August 2026

The first recorded baseline. A named, buildable point on `main` that testers
can be pointed at and that later work is measured against.

**Build:** passes — no type errors, 66 pages generated
**Deploy target:** saqeel.senaei.app

## Why this exists

Work has been landing on `main` for two days with nothing named. Testers were
pointed at a build from yesterday and kept re-finding defects that were already
fixed. A baseline ends that: from here, "is it in?" is answered by a tag rather
than by a conversation.

## What is in B1

**The three permission faults that locked out the whole organisation.**
Creating a compliance change, approving one, and recording an enforcement
decision were all refused for every user, because each check named roles that do
not exist. Fixed and verified live.

**The arrival radius.** No inspection could be carried out anywhere, because the
value that decides whether an inspector has arrived had never been supplied.
Set to 100 metres, ruled by the Product Owner.

**Enforcement decisions enabled.** Supervisors decide. Administrators are
excluded, by ruling, to keep the separation of duties.

**One application.** Administration used a separate shell; the left menu could
vanish. Merged, 564 lines removed. Every administration screen is reachable.

**Confirm-ready no longer refuses a condition already satisfied** — the fault
that stopped an inspector starting a visit that was ready.

**Language.** Database and engineering vocabulary removed from screens business
users read:
- "Loading RLS-scoped data" — the shared loader's default, so 117 screens
- "Deterministic" — five places, four on the Dashboard
- Internal reference numbers beside page titles — 33 screens
- Environment-variable names on switched-off screens
- The Planning status rules screen rewritten so permissions, stages and columns
  say what they mean
- The access screen is "Users & Roles" everywhere, including Arabic

**Presentation.** Inspector screens standardised to one width from seven. Two
table treatments unified. Duplicate Factory 360 routes removed and a
misconfigured route boundary corrected alongside.

## What is NOT in B1, and matters

**The inspection journey does not complete.** Steps 00 to 08 pass on a fresh
chain — plan, approve, release, startup pack, mode, travel, check-in, the
inspection, evidence. **Step 09, submit, is broken:** a fully compliant
inspection is refused, the screen says it is queued, and it never sends. Steps
10 to 12 have never been reached.

The rule behind it was ruled today — a corrective form is required only when a
violation exists — and is recorded in
`CC-SUBMIT-ACTION-FORM-VIOLATION-GATED-20260805`. Not yet built.

**Seventy permission rules are unsatisfiable.** Eleven role names are used
across the platform that do not exist in the database. 58% of the rules that
check a role name only roles that are not real, so they refuse everybody. Three
were found by tripping over them; the rest are known and unfixed. This is the
largest open risk on the platform.

**Administrators are refused Planning.** Ruled today that they should see it.
Not yet built.

**Fifteen reported defects from Sujatha and Sikander** are in progress and not
in B1.

**Jira does not yet stand on its own.** Three of nine requirement documents were
indexed. A tester still cannot work from the stories alone.

**The design file is about a quarter corrected.**

## How to use this baseline

Testers should be told plainly: **the administration, compliance and planning
screens are worth testing. The field journey stops at submit.** Reporting that
submission hangs is not new information.
