## INSP-245 — production/operating-status gate on violation creation

**Confirmed present, and it is a live, structurally central rule — not "Legacy Reference only."** `BC021` (row **INS-BR-045**, inside UC001's main business-rules block, section 5.1) states: for facility status ∈ {Creation (إنشاء), Establishment (تأسيس), Transfer (نقل), Repair (اصلاح), Closed (مغلق), Suspended (متوقف)} — **no violations are approved or recorded** from data-entry mismatches in the Labor, Raw Materials, Products, Machines, and (Arabic-only — see discrepancy below) Spare Parts sections. For facility status = **Production (الانتاج)**, any non-conforming data found in those same sections **is** treated as an actual violation and recorded in the visit's approved violation log. This is enforced through the mandatory "Factory Status According to On-Site Visit" field (with a conditional "Production Status" sub-field shown only when status = Production) that the inspector fills during every visit report. A related but distinct rule, `BC023` (**INS-BR-047**), auto-generates two specific violation types regardless of section (expired license; status/license-phase mismatch) and is not subject to this gate.
> Arabic: *"عند تنفيذ الزيارة على منشأة حالتها (إنشاء / تأسيس /نقل / اصلاح / مغلق / متوقف): لا يتم اعتماد أو تسجيل أي مخالفات ناتجة عن تعبئة بيانات الأقسام التالية: العمالة، المواد الأولية، المنتجات، الآلات، قطع غيار... في حالة (الانتاج): يتم اعتبار أي بيانات مخالفة في الأقسام المذكورة أعلاه مخالفة فعلية ويتم تسجيلها في سجل المخالفات المعتمد للزيارة."*

## Governed values confirmed in source (not invented)

- Official working hours for actual-visit-time calculation: **7:30 AM – 4:30 PM** (`BC013`, INS-BR-037).
- Max attachment size: **5 MB** (`NF004`, INS-BR-011).
- Remote-visit facility eligibility: last **2** approved visits onsite (not self-assessment/remote) **and** compliance score **≥ 50%** (`BC-R001`, INS-BR-057).
- Establishment data-update approval SLA: **60 days**, else system auto-applies the update and issues a violation (`BC008`/`INS2-BR-029` in UC002, restated as `BC001`/`ALT001` in UC003 — `INS2-BR-082`, `INS2-BR-089`).
- Grace-period precedence: Compliance-modified value at visit level takes priority; otherwise the longest grace period among the visit's violations (`BC007`, INS2-BR-028).
- Severe penalties (production-line closure, facility closure, product seizure, destruction) auto-create a mandatory follow-up field visit (`BC005`, INS2-BR-026 — see contradiction #13 above re: the penalty screen's optional checkbox).
- Two governed values left unspecified in the source itself (not invented here): the standard system response-time threshold (`NF003`, "المدة الزمنية القياسية والمعتمدة لدى الوزارة" — no number given) and the inspector task-completion SLA day-count (`BC015`, "المدة الزمنية المحددة (SLA)" — no number given).
